03 / Plan with care

Incentives and tax watch-outs

Plan incentives around the operating model. Requirements differ between Singapore and Malaysia.

Incentive planning

Build the model first.

Malaysia’s targeted JS-SEZ incentives are zone-specific and focus mainly on sectors such as manufacturing, smart logistics and global services. Some schemes target large-scale projects. Smaller investments can still access standard Malaysian investment incentives.

On the Singapore side, the playbook points to grants and incentives from EDB and EnterpriseSG for JS-SEZ-related activities.

SMF JS-SEZ Playbook, p. 15

04 / Plan for people

Passes for staff and business visitors

The playbook appendix describes different routes for expatriate posts, short-term professional work and business travel.

Employment Pass

For expatriate posts

MIDA assesses applications for expatriate posts under Employment Pass categories. Approval depends on the relevant criteria and the availability of expertise among Malaysians.

SMF JS-SEZ Playbook, p. 28

Professional Visit Pass

For temporary professional work

The appendix describes short-term work such as training, consultancy or project work for an overseas company. The pass is for up to 12 months and is not extendable.

SMF JS-SEZ Playbook, pp. 28–29

Investor Pass

For business travellers

The pass allows eligible foreign business travellers to stay for up to 12 months: six months at a time, with a possible further six months subject to requirements.

SMF JS-SEZ Playbook, p. 29
